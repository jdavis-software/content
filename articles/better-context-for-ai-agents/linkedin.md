An AI agent can have your entire repository and still miss the one detail that matters.

The current contract. The caller that depends on an edge case. The test that explains why the code behaves that way.

That is the subject of part three of my engineering series: giving agents the right context, not just more of it.

I cover task-specific evidence, Nx graphs, TypeScript and Go semantic navigation, current worktree state, accepted decisions, and when lexical or vector retrieval actually earns its place.

The goal is not the smallest possible prompt. It is enough relevant evidence to make a justified change—with a clear way to discover more.

There is an interactive example where you can compare a focused packet with a repository dump, introduce a stale contract, remove a required test, and see what the context checks actually catch.

A larger packet is not automatically wrong. A smaller one is not automatically sufficient. And a matching content hash is not permission to access the source.
