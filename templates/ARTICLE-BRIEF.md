# Article brief

## Objective
What should the reader understand or be able to do afterward?

## Audience and takeaway
Who is this for? What is the one argument?

## Evidence
Separate personal observations, verified primary-source facts, illustrative examples and planned work. Store private source material outside the public repository.

## Outline
Use concrete examples, implementation details and meaningful tradeoffs. Avoid fabricated experience, metrics and engagement bait.

## Deliverables
article.md; metadata.json; public sources.json; linkedin.md; necessary reviewed assets; optional editable diagram.

## Review
Exact package fingerprint, validation result, browser checks, public-disclosure check and an explicit decision outside editable metadata. No implicit publication.
